The same two full measures plus pickup are used in every candidate. The phrase
includes the 11:6 eighth-note tuplet, four RH slurs, four LH slurs, fingerings,
three staccato dots, five-flat key signature, accidentals, hairpins, expression,
metronome marking, pedal signs and rests. It was manually transcribed from the
reference, not recognized with OMR. It is an engraving study, not a new edition.

Scale:
The reference's top-staff outer lines are approximately 55 image pixels apart.
Those 55 pixels are displayed as 7 mm. Our native scores use 7 mm staff height
and 174 mm line width, close to this reference system's measured width. No
nonuniform stretching, glyph replacement, or line-weight adjustment is applied
to the reference. Raster softness and source-page skew remain visible. The
comparison aligns the top staff; vertical distances between hands are allowed
to differ because they are part of each engine's output. Page-title design and
complete-page pagination are outside this short-excerpt comparison.

Notation-preserving adapter corrections discovered during this test:
- Put pedal directions in the lower-staff MusicXML stream for musicxml2ly.
- Combine p + espress. and retain italic 2 fingerings in the LilyPond export.
- MuseScore uses no-break layout elements to keep all three measures together.
- MuseScore imported the final hairpin's tuplet endpoint with a tiny timing
  correction (-1/21120 of a whole note). That shifted the final note and caused
  its slur to disappear on native-score reopening, plus an extra end barline.
  Remove that small location and restore the exact 15/22 whole-note hairpin
  span. The fix is scoped to this excerpt and is not a general repair rule.
- Verovio uses an explicit initial MEI system break for single-system layout.
- CairoSVG cannot load the embedded WOFF2 metronome font directly. The script
  converts the same bundled Leipzig text font to local TTF using fontTools and
  exposes it through a project-local Fontconfig file. No font is redesigned.
- Align Verovio's separate p and espress. objects onto the source's shared
  baseline after SVG layout. This does not alter any musical event.

Validation:
The transcription checks measure durations exactly: 3, 6 and 6 quarter notes
in each staff. The final MuseScore native file retains 51 pitched notes and
8 slurs, with a matching MIDI pitch multiset; Verovio MEI retains 51 notes and
8 slurs. Counts are a structural safeguard, not a proof of all musical details.
All four finished comparison pages were also visually checked against the scan.
The 11-note tuplet is represented exactly with integer MusicXML durations at
44 divisions per quarter. Renderer logs and check results are under logs/.

This test makes the remaining aesthetic gap apparent. Henle distributes the
bar widths and shapes long curves differently; choosing a music font alone
will not reproduce those decisions. A future refinement can tune those optical
relationships while keeping this transcription fixed.

TYPOGRAPHY REVISION
The same-passage comparison now uses C059 (URW Century Schoolbook style)
for tempo, expression, fingerings and tuplet numbers. This is a visual
approximation, not an identification or reproduction of Henle's font.
Tempo uses regular serif at approximately 12pt; expressions use italic at
approximately 11pt; fingerings use bold 7pt with the two composer's RH 2s
in bold italic; tuplets use bold italic approximately 8pt. LilyPond's
relative font sizes are tuned separately from the point-size engines.
Metronome figures are smaller than the tempo word. Music-symbol glyphs,
including time signatures, dynamics and pedal signs, retain engine fonts.

LilyPond settings: styles/chopin-typography.ily (both A and B).
MuseScore settings: generated styles/musescore-chopin.mss, with the
role settings and inline metronome size applied by chopin_study.py.
Verovio: SVG typography adapter in chopin_study.py; changes text roles
and tuplet digits while preserving note/metronome music glyphs. The SVG
adapter must run on each export; raw MEI alone does not carry all these
appearance adjustments. Finger italic selection is excerpt-specific.
All font choices use installed free URW fonts and are embedded in PDF.
A now means default engraving geometry with revised typography.
Previous comparison retained at tmp/pdfs/chopin-before-font-adjustment.pdf.

FINGERING REFINEMENT (supersedes fingering settings above)
Fingerings now use a project-local derivative of C059 Bold/Bold Italic,
with horizontal outlines and advances at 88% width and a smaller size
(6.4pt MuseScore/Verovio; -4.4 LilyPond relative size). This reduces the
broad, blunt appearance of the previous full-width bold figures while
retaining enough weight for small print. A regular-weight trial was too
light and was rejected after an enlarged same-scale comparison.
The two italic RH fingerings use the corresponding narrowed italic cut.
No source Henle outlines were traced. This remains an approximation.
The font is rebuilt and installed by chopin_study.py through
fonts/fingering/build_font.py; source license is included there.
Font family: Engraving Fingering. The upright and italic cuts are exposed
as Regular/Italic so engines do not apply additional synthetic bolding.
Tempo/expression/tuplet and music-symbol fonts are unchanged this pass.

TEMPO AND TREBLE-2 REFINEMENT
LilyPond now encodes each italic treble 2 as a Fingering with a font-name
individual tweak, rather than TextScript markup. It consequently inherits
the same -4.4 size and fingering placement rules as the upright digits.
The italic shape is preserved, matching the distinction in the reference.
The previous markup used a different base size and appeared too large.

Larghetto now uses Engraving Tempo, a project-local Nimbus Roman Bold
derivative with 60 font units of additional advance per glyph, giving
sharper serif shapes and more open letter spacing. The heading remains
approximately 12pt; metronome figures retain separate smaller C059 text.
This is a visual approximation, not the original Henle typeface.
fonts/tempo/build_font.py rebuilds/installs the font automatically on render;
its source license is included alongside it. All four treatments use it.

GLYPH-SHAPE REFINEMENT
The C059-based fingering trial is superseded by a fingering-only font
built from LilyPond's Emmentaler-20 numerals. Its open-top 4 and curved
foot on 2 address shapes that the text fonts could not reproduce.
All digits are normalized to 700 units of height and 88% relative width;
italic fingerings use a gentle 0.10 shear. Font family is unchanged. Sizes are 7pt in MuseScore/Verovio and -3.6
in LilyPond to compensate for the different optical metrics.
fonts/fingering/build_font.py is the active reproducible build recipe.
The old C059 recipe is archived as build_c059_trial.py. Emmentaler's
license and embedding notice are stored alongside the current recipe.
This is an adaptation of a free music font, not a tracing of Henle glyphs.
