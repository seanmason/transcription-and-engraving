## Method and limits

Each bar was inspected for both staves, accidentals, bass duration/curve endpoints, clef and meter changes. Enlarged crops were used for ledger lines and accidentals. A second pass checked accidental carry across voices of the same staff, and repeated passages were compared separately. The rendered clean score was compared again with the scan; both full PDFs were visually reviewed. No OMR output, prior automatic pitch labels, or MIDI note counts were treated as source evidence.

Ledger spacing in the print is not uniform outside the staff: count visible ledger lines instead of projecting staff-step distances. The accidental pass changed several initially proposed corrections (notably 12, 14, 18, 26, 27 and 30). The final source files and event inventory contain the adopted readings.
